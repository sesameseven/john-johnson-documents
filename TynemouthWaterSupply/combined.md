# Tynemouth Water Supply (1897)
A digital transcription of the 1897 Tynemouth Water Supply report, originally issued by the Borough Surveyor’s Office and the Medical Officer of Health.  
This repository contains page‑by‑page transcriptions taken from the 12 scanned images, preserved in their original order and formatting. The entire document was scanned.

The purpose of this combined file is to provide a single navigational index linking to each individual page transcription stored in the `transcriptions/` directory.
