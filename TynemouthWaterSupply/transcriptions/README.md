# Tynemouth Water Supply (1897)

This folder contains a digitised transcription of the historical documents relating to the water supply of Tynemouth and North Shields, originally produced by the Municipal Borough of Tynemouth in 1897. The material includes official reports, engineering commentary, and public health assessments concerning the Font water source and the wider water supply challenges of the district.

## Contents

### **Cover Page**
- `cover.md`  
  Overview of the document set, including date, origin, and purpose.

### **Transcriptions**
- `page521.md`  
  Borough Surveyor’s Office report (1 November 1897), outlining the history of the water supply, the inadequacy of the North Shields Waterworks Company, and the Royal Commission’s concerns about the Font source.

- `page522.md`  
  Report by J. Edward Gofton, Medical Officer of Health, discussing the suitability of the Font water, issues of lead solvent action in peaty waters, and comparisons with other towns’ water treatment practices.
