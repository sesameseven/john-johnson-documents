The following table is a comparison of the formulae of various authorities for the calculation of the flow through pipes. The formulae considered are:—

(a) Hazen–Williams.

Q = A.C.r^0.63 . S^0.54 . 0.001^-0.04

Where:—

Q = Flow in cubic feet per second.
A = Area in square feet.
r = Hydraulic radius.
S = Hydraulic gradient.
C = A constant dependent upon the condition of the surface in contact with the water.

(b) Chezy.

Q = A.C.√(r.s)

(c) Eytelwein.

Q = .0785 √(d^5 H / L)

Where:—

D = diameter of pipe in feet.
d = diameter of pipe in inches.
r = Hydraulic Radius = D/4.
H = Head in feet.
L = Length of pipe in feet.
S = Hydraulic gradient = H/L.

The values of C given by Hazen–Williams are C = 130 for a pipe in perfect condition and 100 for a pipe in good condition. In the case of Chezy’s formula:—
