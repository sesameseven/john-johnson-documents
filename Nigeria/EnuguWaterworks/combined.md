# Enugu Waterworks (1928)
A digital transcription of the 1928 Enugu Waterworks Technical Paper No. 1 from the  public works department Nigeria. 
Paper written by J. R. Roberts Assoc. M.Inst. C.E., Water Engineer.  
This repository contains page‑by‑page transcriptions taken from the 14 scanned pages of the document along with 5 pages of  scanned figures.
images. Original document structure has been preserved with some adjustments for the formatting  of data tables.
 The entire document was scanned.

The purpose of this combined file is to provide a single navigational index linking to each individual page transcription stored in the `transcriptions/` directory.
